# CUSTOMER-AND-SALES-ANALYSIS
CUSTOMER AND SALES PERFORMANCE ANALYSIS USING POWER BI

## PROJECT OVERVIEW:
THIS PROJECT ANALYZES CUSTOMER AND SALES  PERFORMANCE USING MICROSOFT POWER BI.THE DASHBOARD PROVIDES INSIGHTS INTO SALES,DISCOUNTS,PROFIT,LOSSES,CUSTOMER SEGMENTS,PRODUCTS AND BUSINESS PERFORMANCE ACROSS DIFFERENT REGIONS,STATES,AND CITIES.


OBJECTIVES: 
- ANALYZE OVERALL SALES AND CUSTOMER PERFORMANCE.
- IDENTITY PROFITABLE AND LOSS-MAKING PRODUCTS.
- EVALUATE PERFORMANCE ACROSS REGIONS,STATES,AND CITIES.
- EXAMINE THE IMPACT OF DISCOUNTS ON BUSINESS PROFITABILITY.
- IDENTITFY KEY BUSINESS PROBLEMS AND PROVIDE RECOMMENDATIONS.
  

KEY FINDINGS
- THE ANALYSIS REVIEWED THAT THE BUSINESS EXPERIENCED SIGNIFICANT LOSSES ACROSS SOME REGIONS,STATES,CITIES,AND PRODUCTS.
- ONE MAJOR FACTORS CONTRIBUTING TO THESE LOSSES WAS HIGH DISCOUNTING.WHILE DISCOUNTS CAN INCREASE SALES,EXCESSIVE DISCOUNTS REDUCED PROFIT MARGINS AND RESULTED IN LOSSES ON SOMES PRODUCTS AND CERTAIN LOCATIONS.
- THE ANALYSIS ALSO IDENTIFIED THAT THE BUSINESS SELLS PRODUCTS THAT ARE CURRENTLY OUT OF TREND AND LOW IN DEMAND.
- THE ANALYSIS ALSO IDENTIFIED DIFFRENCES IN PERFORMANCE ACROSS PRODUCTS AND GEOGRAPHICAL AREAS,HIGHLIGHTING AREAS THAT REQUIRE CLOSER MANAGEMENT AND REVIEW.
  

 TOOLS USED
 - POWER QUERY -DATA CLEANING AND TRANSFORMATION.
 - POWER BI -DATA VISUALIZATION AND DASHBOARD DEVELOPMENT.
  

 RECOMMENDATIONS
 
- REVIEW AND REDUCE EXCESSIVE DISCOUNTS, ESPECIALLY ON LOSS-MAKING PRODUCTS.
- SET DISCOUNTS LIMITS BASED ON PRODUCTS PROFITABILITY.
- REVIEW UNDERPERFORMING REGIONS,STATES,AND CITIES.
- REGULARLY MONITOR SALES, PROFIT AND DISCOUNT PERFORMANCE USING THE DASHBOARD.
- REVIEW PRODUCTS THAT ARE OUT OF TREND OR EXPERIENCING LOW DEMAND AND AVOID STOCKING THEM HEAVILY.
- INSTEAD OF APPLYING EXCESSIVE DISCOUNTS THAT RESULT IN LOSSES,CONSIDER BETTER INVENTORY MANAGEMENT AND CONTROLLED CLEARANCE STRATEGIES FOR OUTDATED PRODUCTS.


CONCLUSIONS

THE POWER BI ANALYSIS PROVIDES A CLEAR VIEW OF THE BUSINESS SALES AND PRODUCT PERFORMANCE.ALTHOUGH THE BUSINESS GENERATES GOOD SALES ,EXCESSIVE DISCOUNTING IS NEGAIVELY AFFECTING PROFITABILITY.A BETTER DISCOUNT MANAGEMENT AND CLOSE MONITORING OF LOSS-MAKING PRODUCTS AND LOCATIONS CAN HELP IMPROVE OVERALL BUSINESS PERFORMANCE AND PROFITABILITY.ONE DRIVER OF THE HEAVY DISCOUNTING IS THE PRESENCE OF PRODUCTS THAT ARE NO LONGER IN TREND OR HAVE REDUCED CUSTOMER DEMAND,HOWEVER THE EXCESSIVE DISCOUNTS USED TO MOVE THESE PRODUCTS OUT APPEAR TO HAVE CONTRIBUTED TO GREAT LOSSES.
THE BUSINESS SHOULD ADOPT BETTER INVENTORY AND PRODUCT MANAGEMENT STRATEGIES.IDENTIFY DECLINING PRODUCTS EARLIER,CONTROLLING STOCK LEVELS,AND FOCUSING ON PRODUCTS WITH STRONGER DEMAND CAN HELP REDUCE LOSSES AND IMPROVE OVERALL PROFITABILTY.
